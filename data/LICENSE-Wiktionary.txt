The dictionaries WinDict downloads for languages other than English are built
from Wiktionary, by its contributors:

  Italiano: Wikizionario, the Italian Wiktionary (https://it.wiktionary.org),
  as extracted by wiktextract (https://kaikki.org/itwiktionary/).

Wiktionary's text is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
and so is each dictionary built from it.

Changes: the entries were limited to the dictionary's language, cut down to
their definitions, examples, synonyms, opposites and inflected forms, and
converted into WinDict's format by tools/build_wiktionary.py.
